About

Shirin Pasqual is an Austrian director and filmmaker working across documentary, fashion and commissioned film.

Her work explores human behaviour, social rituals and the peculiarities of everyday life. Drawn to unusual subjects, subcultures and environments, she is interested in the tension between private identity and public performance.

Moving between observation and constructed scenarios, her research-led approach begins with finding people, places and situations that can become the starting point for a film.

She develops projects from initial concept and research through to casting and direction, working across independent, editorial, fashion and commercial contexts.

Her work includes collaborations with Sabukaru, Bianco Bianco, VICE, Highsnobiety, Nike, adidas, ASICS and GATA, among others.

Originally from Vorarlberg, Austria, she spent three years living and working in Tokyo. She is currently based in Austria and available internationally.
