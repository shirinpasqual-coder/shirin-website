Shirin Pasqual (b. 1999, Vorarlberg, Austria) is a director and filmmaker
working across documentary, fashion and commissioned film.

Her work explores human behaviour, social rituals and the ways people construct and perform identity. Moving between documentary observation and constructed situations, she is drawn to people, subcultures and environments that reveal the tension between authenticity and performance.
