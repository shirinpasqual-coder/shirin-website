Shirin Pasqual (b. 1999, Vorarlberg, Austria) is a filmmaker and visual artist.

Her work moves between documentation and observation,
drawn to the uncanny and the threshold between staging and authenticity.
The body as object, identity under pressure, performance,
and detachment are recurring coordinates within her practice.
